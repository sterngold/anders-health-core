# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome different backgrounds, abilities, bodies, ages, and health contexts.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Protect privacy and never pressure anyone to disclose health information.
- Correct errors openly and distinguish evidence from opinion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, publishing
private health information, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
