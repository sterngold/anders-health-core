# Health disclaimer

Anders Health Core is general-purpose software for organizing and analysing
self-tracked data. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical
advice, diagnosis, treatment, medication guidance, or emergency support.

Data quality, consumer devices, source changes, missing observations, and
analytical choices can all produce misleading results. Observational
associations do not establish causation. Do not use an output from this project
as the sole basis for a health or treatment decision.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions. Contact
local emergency services for urgent or emergency care.
